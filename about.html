ABOUT TASKTAP

Helping communities help each other.

TaskTap is a local task marketplace designed to make everyday help easier to find while creating opportunities for people to earn from their time and skills.

OUR PURPOSE

Make local help more accessible.

01

Connect nearby people

Bring task posters and task-doers together around practical, everyday needs in their community.

02

Make opportunities visible

Give people a straightforward way to discover small paid tasks that match their skills and availability.

03

Build stronger trust

Use clear task information, completion records, and community reputation to make interactions easier to understand.

WHAT WE VALUE

Simple by design. Community by nature.

Local

Start with the communities people already know and live in.

Clear

Keep task expectations, compensation, and deadlines visible.

Responsible

Respect privacy and encourage safer, transparent interactions.

Inclusive

Make it easier for people to contribute skills in ways that fit their circumstances.
