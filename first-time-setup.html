Start with safer habits

TaskTap works best when members protect their accounts and keep coordination inside the platform.

Keep your exact address private

Only use the approximate barangay on your public profile. Share a meeting point only when needed for a task.

Never share credentials

TaskTap will never ask you to post a password, OTP, bank login, or full payment-card number in a task or message.

✓
Use TaskTap messages

Keep task details and coordination in the platform when possible so there is a clear conversation record.

✓
Report anything unsafe

You can report a task or member if something feels suspicious, abusive, unsafe, or inappropriate.
