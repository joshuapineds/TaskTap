Admin Guide

Quick instructions for the TaskTap administration prototype.

⌕Ctrl K
ⓘ

Prototype guide — The admin pages use sample data. Actions such as suspend, archive, restore, and save are demonstrations and don’t change real accounts or platform records.

01

Members and profiles

Find members and review their sample account information.

  1. Use User Management to search by name, email, or member ID and filter by role, verification, or status.
  2. Open a member row to review their profile, verification state, task history, reviews, reports, and sample activity timeline.
  3. Demo status actions update only the current page’s sample data.
Open User Management →
02

Tasks

Search listings, inspect details, and review demo task status.

  1. Filter the Task Management list by status, category, barangay, or search text.
  2. Open a task to see the poster, listing amount, report context, and sample history.
  3. Pause, resume, or archive controls are local demonstrations; task budgets are not payment transactions.
Open Task Management →
03

Categories

Maintain the categories shown by the category prototype.

  1. Search or filter active and inactive sample categories.
  2. Add or edit a category from the page controls, then use the status action to deactivate or reactivate it.
  3. Edits remain in the category page and do not update member task forms or other pages.
Open Category Management →
04

Reports and moderation

Review a sample report queue and its linked context.

  1. Start with high-priority and open cases, then open a report to read the summary and context.
  2. Follow links to the related sample member or task when more context is needed.
  3. Review, resolve, dismiss, or reopen actions only update the report page’s sample state. They don’t contact anyone.
Open Report Management →
05

Payment records

Review information members entered about payments.

  1. Search and filter by payment status or method, then open a record to inspect its notes.
  2. Use review flags to mark sample entries that need attention.
  3. TaskTap doesn’t process, hold, or transfer money; the page is a record ledger only.
Open Payment Records →
06

Archives

Find retained task, member, and report records.

  1. Choose a record type, search, and sort by archive or review date.
  2. Open a record to inspect its reason and source-page link.
  3. Restore marks the archive entry as restored in the archive demo only; it doesn’t reactivate or republish the original record.
Open Archives →
07

Barangay coverage

Review service area sample data and coverage signals.

  1. Search by barangay or coordinator, filter by coverage status, or sort by task and member counts.
  2. Select a map tile or choose Edit to update a sample area; use Add barangay to create a temporary entry.
  3. The coverage view and area counts are illustrative, not official boundaries or live availability.
Open Barangay Management →
08

Activity log

Explore the prewritten administrative event examples.

  1. Filter by date, section, outcome, or search terms.
  2. Open an event to view its actor, related record, timestamp, and sample context.
  3. Export downloads the currently filtered sample events. The page is not a live or authoritative audit log.
Open Activity Log →
09

Settings

Adjust the settings page’s sample workspace preferences.

  1. Use the section links to jump between General, Notifications, Security, and Localization.
  2. Save changes to update the demo page session, or restore the defaults to reset its controls.
  3. Settings don’t change real security, notifications, language, or platform configuration.
Open Settings →
10

What this prototype does not do

Use this project to review frontend flow and presentation.

  • It doesn’t connect to a server, database, payment provider, messaging channel, or identity service.
  • Most lists and actions are isolated sample data; changing one page usually doesn’t update another page.
  • Don’t treat sample accounts, reports, archive entries, location counts, or audit events as real records.
